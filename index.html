03 / THE THING I NEVER SAY PROPERLY
dear you,♡ 18. ♡

A friendship that stayed.

I've always been someone who's used to moving on from friendships. Every class, a new friend. Next year, another. And the year after that, another. It wasn't like I never had friends; catching up with those friendships after a long time was just never my thing. And honestly, no one else really did either. Low-key, I'm glad I never had to compromise my self-respect to keep them.

Until you happened.

The one friendship that stayed through no messages, no calls, months of no meetings, no catch-ups, no hangouts. A friendship that stayed. Even after months of no contact, we'd look for each other in any crowd. That assurance, somehow, killed the anxious feeling of being abandoned that lived in me.

A friendship without formalities or pretentiousness. I never felt like I had to be something, stay relevant, be fun, or prove that I deserved a place in your life. You taught me that some things are meant to stay despite everything. They don't need to prove themselves. They don't need to always be present. They're simply meant to last.

A friendship that softened every insecurity. A friendship that made me believe that as long as I have you somewhere back there, I don't need to be scared of losing every other friendship. Even if I lose everyone, at least someone will be there—authentically, honestly, without pretending, and without making me pretend either.

I always pretended not to give a damn with you. I'm gratefully dropping the pretended part. Because I know that somehow, in the end, we'll find our way back. Stay when it's hard. Stay when it's not fun. Stay when there's nothing special about staying, and nothing special to stay for.

“Some things are meant to stay.
They don't have to prove it.”

Our late-night talks while stargazing will forever be moments I treasure. With this friendship, for the first time, I didn't feel abnormal, outcasted, or left out. You and Abeer gave me confidence. You are my confidence.

We might not talk all the time now, and that's okay. It's unbelievable how that inferiority complex has turned into something a little like a superiority complex. Thank you for quieting that fear of being left alone in the end—the one that lived so deeply in me.

For me, you'll always be the star on the darkest nights. Whatever life throws at us during the day, at midnight we'll be there to talk about it and make it feel like “just a part of the process.”

To the one I'll exchange luxury cars with at 28—happy 18teen birthday! (Hopefully. Manifesting it.)

I know, I know. It's your birthday and I've written about me and what you make me feel. But I guess that's the best part of having you: you're the one person who listens to me. What can I do?

I won't wish big things for you only today. I'll do it every day instead. And don't worry—in every delusional dream of mine,May we buy our dream house and car together and ASAP! , I dream of you being right there beside me too. You'll make it. I'm damn, damn × 100 sure. Actually.
